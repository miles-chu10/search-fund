## Source and fixture notes

- The working Exa surface was the installed Codex Exa app (`mcp__codex_apps__exa_*`). The separate legacy Exa endpoint returned `401 Invalid API key`; this did not block the app-backed search/fetch workflow.
- “Accessible” means Exa fetched the public canonical page on 2026-08-03. It does **not** prove the seller is responsive, the deal is available, or the claims are accurate.
- For fixture design, preserve `marketplace`, `listingId`, `canonicalUrl`, `firstSeenAt`, `lastSeenAt`, `sourcePublishedAt/sourceUpdatedAt`, and `availabilityClaim` separately. Do not turn the retrieval date into a publication/update date.
- Preserve the source’s financial label (`SDE`, `cash flow`, `income`, or `EBITDA`). Never map one label to another without an explicit normalization rule and an `inference` flag.
- Demo defaults should keep `currentAvailability`, `sellerFinancingTerms`, `operatorReplacementCost`, `customerConcentration`, `contractAssignability`, and `managementDepth` unknown unless the source states them. A daily refresh should mark missing/blocked pages for review rather than silently deleting them.
