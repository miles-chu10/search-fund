# Packet 01: Live sourcing

## Objective

Produce a current public-listing fixture for an evidence-backed acquisition-sourcing demo.

## Context

Use Exa for primary discovery and canonical page fetches. Public listing claims remain unverified leads.

## Ownership

The research worker owns only `results/01-live-sourcing.md`. It must not edit application, test, or documentation files and must preserve concurrent changes.

## Do

- Find 8–12 plausible US acquisition leads.
- Preserve source marketplace, listing ID, canonical URL, retrieval date, raw financial label, and explicit unknowns.
- Separate sourced facts from screening inference.
- Reject sold, closed, blocked, and probable duplicate candidates.

## Do Not

- Contact brokers or sellers, access gated materials, or recommend an acquisition.
- Invent current availability, financials, financing, management, or transition facts.

## Expected Output

`results/01-live-sourcing.md` with a retained fixture set, rejection log, provenance notes, and limitations.

## Verification

Every retained row must have a directly fetched canonical URL and retrieval date; missing fields stay unknown.
