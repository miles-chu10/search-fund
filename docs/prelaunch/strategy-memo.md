# Strategy memo: 12-week search-readiness lane

**Recommendation status:** hypothesis, not an approved thesis  
**Recommended starting lane:** self-funded-size preparation  
**Decision owners:** Miles for strategy and personal limits; qualified professionals for legal, tax, securities, broker-dealer, accounting, and lending questions

## Recommendation

Use the next 12 weeks to pressure-test a smaller, self-funded-size acquisition thesis without applying for financing, contacting external parties, declaring SBA eligibility, or choosing a permanent capital path. This is the most reversible starting point because it tests thesis quality, sourcing discipline, evidence gaps, operator fit, and downside math before depending on an unverified investor network or transaction.

Traditional search and independent sponsor remain open alternatives. The recommendation advances only after Miles decides the private runway, time, risk, and target-size gates in [`contracts/prelaunch-os.json`](../../contracts/prelaunch-os.json).

## User-owned decision gates

The strategy cannot become decision-ready until Miles decides or explicitly defers all ten inputs:

1. part-time preparation or full-time operator role;
2. geography and relocation constraints;
3. included and excluded industries;
4. SDE or EBITDA basis and range;
5. enterprise-value range;
6. required characteristics and hard exclusions;
7. private personal runway and protected reserve;
8. weekly time commitment;
9. debt, guarantee, concentration, and loss limits; and
10. traditional, self-funded, or independent-sponsor path.

Raw bank, tax, brokerage, credit, or account data must not be stored in this repository. Only user-approved derived ranges or decisions belong in the operating contract.

## Continue / pause rule

- **Continue** the preparation cycle when source coverage is visible, every advanced lead has an evidence-linked reason, work fits the approved time box, and no approval gate was bypassed.
- **Pause** if private strategy/path inputs remain unresolved, a source is repeatedly blocked without visible coverage status, sensitive data is at risk, or workload exceeds the agreed capacity twice.
- **Stop or change paths** when the personal downside exceeds Miles's chosen limit or later qualified review rejects a proposed structure, communication, compensation model, or financing path.
