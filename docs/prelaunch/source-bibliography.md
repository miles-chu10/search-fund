## Claim hygiene

- Stanford's aggregate results may be described only as historical performance of its covered core U.S./Canadian population.
- A loan amount below SBA's program maximum does not establish eligibility, approval, terms, collateral, guarantee, equity injection, or repayment capacity.
- SEC materials identify pathways and risk factors; they do not authorize a raise, communication, finder role, fee, or exemption.
- IRS and FTC sources identify diligence and reporting topics; qualified professionals own transaction-specific conclusions.
- Industry surveys describe respondents and closed transactions; they are not default economics, availability, or expected performance.
