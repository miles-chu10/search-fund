## Eight-minute employer demo

1. State the boundary: ten dated, unverified public leads; no current availability, financing, or investment claim.
2. Edit acquisition criteria and show deterministic thesis gates, fixed weights, caps, and no SDE/EBITDA conversion.
3. Open a memo and trace a sourced fact, calculation, inference, unknown, and canonical URL.
4. Open **Readiness** and show the recommended hypothesis, ten user-owned inputs, and three path stop/continue gates.
5. Show exact KPIs, approval gates, priority risks, the privacy-safe diligence index, and draft-only templates with no send control.
6. End on the source register and distinguish automated validation, target-user usability, demand, clients, and outcomes.

## Target-user validation plan

With Miles's approval, recruit separately: two current/recent searchers, one ETA investor or accelerator operator, one SMB acquisition adviser, and one acquisition-minded operator. Do not contact anyone from this project task.

Ask participants to:

1. define a usable thesis without favorable defaults;
2. explain a lead's cap and next evidence request;
3. interpret a KPI denominator and coverage failure;
4. find the proper diligence folder without exposing gated material; and
5. explain why a capital output is unavailable or non-decision-grade.

Capture task completion, time, wrong turns, confidence before/after, and exact feedback. An iteration bar is 4/5 participants completing 4/5 tasks without coaching, with everyone correctly distinguishing a score from a recommendation and a fixture from current availability. Passing supports usability iteration only; it does not prove willingness to pay, market size, product-market fit, revenue, or acquisition outcomes.

## Productized-service boundary

The `Search Readiness Sprint` may deliver:

- client-owned thesis decisions and exclusions;
- a configured private/local workflow;
- provenance and data-hygiene setup;
- scorecard calibration within a versioned rubric;
- weekly review facilitation;
- a diligence-request index; and
- scenario templates populated only from client-provided or cited data.

It excludes deal recommendations, valuation/fairness opinions, legal/tax/securities advice, broker/intermediary activity, investor solicitation, lender placement or eligibility promises, QoE/audit, confidential-document hosting, outbound sending, negotiation, LOI drafting, and transaction commitments. Client and qualified professionals retain every external and go/no-go decision.
